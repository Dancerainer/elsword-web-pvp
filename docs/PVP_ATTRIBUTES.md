# PvP attributes

The twelve profession presets define equipment options, selected passives and mode rules. Resolve equipment inputs first, apply profession and buff channels independently, and take a snapshot of the attack that actually produced the hit. A later skill or branch must not supply an earlier hit's coefficients.

Critical damage starts at a multiplier of 1.5 before applicable equipment and skill adjustments. Additional damage uses the relevant attack channel and the recorded motion and skill multipliers. Apply it as a separate contribution after ordinary damage, defense, mitigation and critical processing. Preserve the distinction between an equipment option's nominal socket value and its already-converted final percentage.

Online matches initialize MP and awakening using the selected match rule. Practice can explicitly prepare resources. Reconnect and restore consume the saved state. The reaction-speed rule caps a positive final speed adjustment for a hurt action while retaining applicable negative modifiers. Keep product rules distinct from original-client measurements.
